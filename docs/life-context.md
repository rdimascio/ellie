# Proactive context matching

`ProactivityEngine` connects explicit context signals to authorized open life records. It supports a shopping-store signal, a location observation, a reported price, and an upcoming-event preparation check. Signals are observations rather than instructions or credentials.

Shopping matches require a need with `data.store` or `data.stores`. Location additionally needs a saved place with `latitude`, `longitude`, and an optional `radiusMeters`; both measurement accuracy and distance must fit that place. Positions and offers more than fifteen minutes old are ignored, as are future-dated observations. Raw coordinates are not retained by this engine.

Prices match the need's `budget` or `targetPrice` and its stated currency. Notifications describe the reporting source rather than claiming independent retailer verification. Preparation checks look for timed events within the next 48 hours and all-day events today or in the next two local calendar days. Timed strings must include an explicit offset or `Z`; all-day dates use the effective profile zone and expire at its next actual day boundary. The shared [calendar helpers](life-time.md) handle skipped midnights and daylight-saving transitions. Completed, cancelled or expired needs and records with invalidated source provenance do not generate new suggestions. Existing preparation notices are hidden when their linked event is postponed beyond the preparation window, removed or no longer active.

The engine honors `proactiveSuggestions: false` (with legacy `proactive` fallback) and structured `quietHours: { enabled: true, start: 22, end: 7 }` in the effective settings, using the selected `timeZone`. It emits at most three matches per signal and retains a per-record cooldown across restart. The cooldown update and notification creation commit in one transaction. Results become scoped feedback records with `notification: true`, an explanation, a related-record link, and expiry. The host can show or dismiss them.

`PreparationMonitor` performs an immediate local check when the Life listener starts, then checks every fifteen minutes while the service is running. Each pass refreshes the actor's authorized scopes and evaluates at most eight, rotating across larger memberships. It uses the same preference, quiet-hour, provenance and cooldown rules as explicit checks. It does not call a model, observe other applications, send a message or keep the computer awake. Stop it before closing stores or beginning personal reset; its admission callback also prevents work during a pending reset. Status reports counts rather than private titles or source text. Missed intervals during sleep or shutdown are not replayed in a burst; startup checks current commitments.

Tests use synthetic places and needs and verify freshness, relevance, private scope, currency/budget constraints, cooldown persistence and completion suppression. No native geofence subscription, automatic cross-app observation, independent retailer inventory feed, or unattended purchasing is enabled by this package. Those are future signal providers behind explicit device or connector permissions.

## Personal context for model replies

`selectModelWorld` supplies a small, read-only selection of people, birthdays, needs, goals, places, routines and commitments from the selected space. It searches four compact windows of fifty records, ranks matching titles and previews, and loads at most eight matching records plus four directly related records. A gift need can therefore bring its linked person's interests even when that person is older than the compact contact window. Private records do not join a group-context request.

The projection admits bounded descriptive fields, explicit time values and whole notes up to 1,200 characters. Longer notes are marked unavailable; stale source-derived records and arbitrary data fields are excluded. Raw coordinates are excluded. Model transport admits whole projected records within a separate 8-KiB allowance and the shared 64-KiB context ceiling. The model receives selection/omission flags: this is partial context, not a complete agenda or an exhaustive search.

These saved observations personalize advice; they never authorize an action or establish current store prices, stock, discounts, restaurant hours or live location. Tests verify scope revocation, linked-person selection, Unicode matching, whole qualifications, bounded reads under large unrelated source collections and refusal to turn a stored instruction into a write.
